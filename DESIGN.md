# Design / Intelligence — art direction
Concept: One maker moves between composing an experience and understanding a system. The home page is an editorial folio: project imagery is the principal visual in Design, and a real retrieval pipeline is the principal visual in Intelligence. The bottom switch changes the whole folio; the 3D well is a separate Work destination.

Design system: near-black #050404, bone #f2ede4, Prussian-blue #003153, amber #edac56 reserved for active state and one technical endpoint. Bodoni Moda handles expressive display; Jost handles body and navigation. One clean grid breaks for the large project image and the pale full-bleed interlude.

Signature moment: the persistent Design / Intelligence switch moves a solid amber carriage, changes the maker identity, headline, lead artwork, project set, descriptions, contact and Work route. It keeps both modes labeled. It is keyboard operable and reduced-motion safe. Sound stays optional. Native scrolling is retained.

Work: five design projects and seven AI projects remain accessible through the full DOM index. The Three.js well is linked from Work only. Its curved screens use real project imagery and project-specific diagrams, with vertical image scrubbing, screen extraction, an observer, and a ring-light ascent. Continuous video recordings have not been supplied; do not present paired stills as footage.

Reference reasoning: Awwwards scores design, usability, creativity and content; one recognizable concept with real work matters more than scattered effects. Carter Ogunsola's awarded portfolio makes its own body of work and print-shop metaphor the interface. This folio transfers the principle that work must be the image, without reproducing the print-shop treatment or wheel.

Checks still required after deployment: real mobile layout at 390px, keyboard order, console errors, font loading, image loading, WebGL performance, reduced motion and cold-load metrics. No award score or speed target is claimed without measurement.

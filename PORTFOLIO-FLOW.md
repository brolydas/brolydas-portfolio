# Six-part portfolio flow

Kind: an independent designer and developer portfolio with design and machine-learning modes.
Concept: visitors see the work, bring a problem, inspect the capabilities behind it, and contact the maker.
Voice: direct first-person project descriptions; no testimonials, customer logos, traffic or commercial results are invented.

Order: Hero with CTA; Selected Work; Project Inquiry; About and project-backed skills; Project Proof; Direct Contact.
Social proof is limited to inspectable artifacts because no verified third-party endorsements were provided. The proof section therefore offers working demos and source rather than claiming external approval.

Art direction preserves the user's approved Space Grotesk / DM Sans, carbon and white, consistent gutters and glass dock. The orange Pepper Club image remains the bright hero moment. New sections use open capability rows and linked project evidence, with no ornamental numbering, badges, repeated arrows, italic headline accents or scroll fades.

Motion: one Motion Canvas composition in Project Inquiry transforms a wireframe into an interface. Native scrolling scrubs its authored timeline using GSAP ScrollTrigger. The fallback is a complete inline SVG interface; controls become available after runtime initialization. No text is hidden, pinned or faded on scroll. A manual range, pause control, visibility suspension and reduced-motion still provide control. The build bundles Motion Canvas 3.17.2 and GSAP 3.13.0 locally; there is no runtime CDN requirement. The Vercel build assembles static output without node_modules or authoring tools.

Requested Taste and Awwwards guidance was reviewed upstream. The existing approved design and the user's explicit rules take precedence over conflicting kit defaults. This is an integration into an existing portfolio, not a claim to have run the complete Taste template/deal/gate workflow or earned an award. No unmeasured performance score is claimed.

Verification: existing catalog/game/export and portfolio suites remain. The new browser suite checks section order, both modes' capability/proof links, native CTA navigation, Motion Canvas rendering and scrubbing, GSAP scroll progress, pause, reduced motion, missing-runtime fallback, responsive overflow and accessible contact. Screenshots and reports are CI artifacts.
